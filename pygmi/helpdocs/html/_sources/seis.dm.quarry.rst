Remove Quarry Events
--------------------
This script allows for the removal of quarry events from seismology data. It is based on the paper by Wiemer and Baer (2000).

References
^^^^^^^^^^

  Wiemer, S. & Baer, M. (2000) Mapping and removing quarry blast events from seismicity catalogues. Bulletin of the Seismological Society of America. (April), 525–530. [online]. Available from: http://www.bssaonline.org/content/90/2/525.short (Accessed 8 November 2013).
